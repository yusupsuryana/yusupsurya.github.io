02 / About

Beyond the CAD model.

Good design is not just what looks right. It is what works in the real world.

I’m a medical device product developer with experience across the full product lifecycle — from ideation and design development to prototyping, manufacturing preparation, and packaging.

I pay close attention to the gap between a design concept and the realities of production. That means considering function, usability, material choices, assembly, quality requirements, and manufacturing constraints early in the process.

Alongside hands-on engineering, I have led project and design teams, coordinated cross-functional stakeholders, and helped move projects toward key technical and production milestones.

My work is grounded in practical problem-solving: align the team, clarify the requirements, make informed design decisions, and keep the product moving toward a solution that can be built and used.
